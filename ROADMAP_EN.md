# IA'GS roadmap

## Available in v0.1.0

- Native photo preparation, Apple Vision segmentation and editable masks.
- Camera grouping, Plus / Pro, local reconstruction and measured training progress.
- Starfield UI, named tasks, search/history, accent colors, languages and local backgrounds.
- Gaussian PLY viewing, editing and export; a macOS test installer.

## Next

- Broader device, camera and material coverage, including a complete Pro training validation.
- Better review and failure feedback for difficult segmentation cases.
- Developer ID signing and Apple notarization.
- Evaluate additional image formats after orientation, metadata and pixel-preservation tests.

These are directions, not release-date commitments. Video input, Windows, Linux and generative photo rewriting remain outside the current scope.
